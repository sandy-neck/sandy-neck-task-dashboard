## What I concluded
1. **The story is transactions, not dollars.** 14 orders on a 465 day is the anomaly; the $806 follows from it. Two candidate causes: reduced open hours / staffing, or a genuine footfall drop. I cannot distinguish them from the data, so I asked rather than asserted. Medium confidence that it is operational rather than demand — because the AOV went *up*, which is what you'd expect if the store was open fewer hours and only committed buyers came through, and what you would not expect if visitors had simply thinned out (thinner crowds should keep the ice-heavy low-ticket mix roughly intact).
2. **The flat weekday ceiling holds as a ceiling, not a floor.** Five days at ~$1,250 said midweek is capped. Aug 12 says it can also come in far under. So the correct statement is "Mon–Thu in peak can't exceed ~$1,300 no matter how good the day is," not "Mon–Thu reliably does $1,250." That's a meaningful weakening of how I've been using it for forecasting.
3. **The week-ahead surplus of $114 is fiction, same as last week's $207.** Four midweek days priced $1,864–$1,955 against a measured ceiling of ~$1,300. That's ~$2.4k of phantom revenue. If Aug 12 is repeatable rather than one-off, worse. Told BJ to read the week as ~$2.5k light with weekend the only headroom. Medium-high confidence.
4. **TikTok reach is collapsing on a clean monotonic six-week trend.** The dollars are irrelevant and always were; the point is the channel the off-season plan leans on is losing distribution while it's being neglected. Decision needed in the next few weeks, not October.
