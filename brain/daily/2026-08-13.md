## Structural notes
- POS share printed at 97.0% this week only because three older online orders aged out of the trailing window. Channel-share percentages on a 7-day window are unstable when the denominator's small side has 3–6 orders. Don't report share moves under ~3 points as meaningful.
- Order count is a better midweek health signal than revenue. Revenue is dominated by whether one $135 umbrella or $98 chair went out the door; transactions track how many people walked in. When the two diverge, believe the transactions.
