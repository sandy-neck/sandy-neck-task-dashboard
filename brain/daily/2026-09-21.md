## Structural notes
- Late shoulder is one to two trading days a week. Calendar-weekday comparisons are noise; the only comparable set is Saturday-to-Saturday, and even that is now three points.
- `expected` from the curve is meaningless on closed or barely-open days and must never lead. `required` stays meaningful but has been unavailable for most of the last week.
- The week-ahead `expected_week_total` assumes seven open days and should be manually overridden every time in this phase. Third override in four runs — this ought to be gated on open days.
- 7-day session percentages have been junk for three straight weeks. Always quote the median daily figure and name what is sitting in the window.
- TikTok's share of order count rises mechanically as POS falls. Denominator effect, not a channel win.
- The 7-day in-store board carries no ranking information at single-order volume — read mix only.
- AOV is now the most informative in-store metric, not revenue. At two to twenty orders a day, total revenue is dominated by whether one apparel buyer walked in; basket composition is the stable signal.
