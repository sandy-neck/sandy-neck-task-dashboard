## Structural notes
- Saturdays carry the week. Aug 8 alone was ~35% of its week's in-store revenue; Aug 1 was ~22%. Any week without a strong Saturday is lost.
- Weekday revenue clusters at a level set by how many people are on the Cape that week, not by day quality within the week. The cluster level moves week to week (~$700 late July, ~$1,250 first week of August); the flatness within a week is the consistent part.
