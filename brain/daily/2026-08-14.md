## Structural notes
- Midweek Mon–Thu at peak season caps around $1,250 regardless of sky; Fri–Sun respond to conditions. Established, do not re-explain.
- In-store is 94–97% of revenue every week. Never news.
- August is the year's biggest month. Never news.
- The expectation curve's weather term is effectively inert Mon–Thu and roughly calibrated Fri–Sun. Treat % -of-expected as meaningful mainly on weekends.
- Same-day expected and week-ahead expected use inconsistent paths (noted Aug 11: Tue 475 → $1,008 same-day vs Tue 470 → $1,955 forward). Do not compare the two.
- Traffic headline percentages are dominated by whether the Aug 8–10 weekend spike sits inside the 7-day window. Always read the daily series, never the headline.
