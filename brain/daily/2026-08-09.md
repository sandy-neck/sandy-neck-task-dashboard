## What I concluded
1. **The flat-ceiling / "expectation curve is too high" read needs revising.** Aug 8 came in at 79% of a Saturday-adjusted $3,917; Aug 9 came in at 134% of $2,356. Same store, adjacent days, near-identical scores, wildly different % of expected. The variance is in the denominator, not the business — the day-of-week factor is over-correcting Saturdays and under-correcting Sundays. Medium confidence. Curve is parked; not touching it, but I should stop treating % of expected as comparable across weekdays until it's refit.
2. **Sunday is not a weekday.** That thread is settled in the direction opposite to my lean. Aug 2 (395) $859 and Jul 26 (425) $1,159 looked like weekday-band days, but they were mid-range scores; at 450 Sunday produced $3,153, above the Saturday at the same score. High confidence the "Sunday behaves like a weekday" hypothesis is dead. Lower confidence on what replaces it — possibly Sunday is score-elastic in a way weekdays aren't.
3. **Basket composition, not traffic, drove the day.** 23 fewer orders than Saturday, $61 more revenue. Umbrella/chair/board sales are ~$1,064 of the week's top-8 alone. This is the first day where the ice-drags-basket story ran in reverse — and it suggests late-peak buyers are replacing gear rather than topping up consumables. Worth watching whether this holds through the week.
4. **The main actionable item is a stock check on beach hard goods**, framed with the calendar. 12 days of peak left. Anything with a lead time over ~7 days is answer #2 (let it sell out). I flagged honestly that I don't have those vendors' lead times.
5. Left out: pacing, TikTok, Green Head Guys, deflators, the raw record-day narration. BJ worked both days.
