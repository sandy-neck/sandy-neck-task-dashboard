## Structural notes
- Saturdays carry the week: Aug 8 ($3,092) was ~29% of the surrounding week's in-store revenue; Aug 1 ~22%.
- Within a strong vacation week, weekday revenue clusters near $1,200–1,300 across a 420–460 score band. In a weak week (late July) the same cluster sat near $700. The cluster level moves week to week; the flatness within a week is the durable part.
- Order count is *not* flat within that band — it ranged 22 to 57 across Aug 4–7. Revenue flatness is achieved through offsetting basket size. Do not use order count as a proxy for a day's strength.
- Ice is ~$3 a bag and ~2.4 bags a ticket; a heavy-ice day mechanically drags AOV without anything being wrong.
- Online/TikTok together are ~5% of revenue at peak and move barely at all with weather. In-store is the whole story until Labor Day.
