The only one with even weak evidence is the Saturday morning event (pilates, Aug 8). Sat Aug 15 (score 450) is the right target.

So the August window is closed regardless — that decision is already made and can't be unmade. The reason to order anyway is that deflators aren't perishable, sell into shoulder-season oversand traffic, and map onto the Jeep online audience. This is a *different* case from the fly repellent even though the lead-time math looks similar. Confidence: medium — hinges on whether oversand traffic actually continues into Sep–Oct, which I have not verified, and on MOQ, which I don't know.
