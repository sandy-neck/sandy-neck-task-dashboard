## Structural notes
- Weekday in-store revenue in a strong vacation week sits at $1,200–1,300 regardless of day quality across at least a 420–485 band. The cluster *level* moves week to week (late July sat near $700); the flatness within a week is the reliable part.
- Weekend response to day quality is real; weekday response is not. Weather forecasting is therefore only useful for Fri–Sun planning.
- Trailing-day session counts always undercount and fill in within 24–48 hours. Never read the last day of the traffic series.
- AOV and order count move inversely day to day; low-order days show high AOV mechanically. Don't read AOV without the order count beside it.
- Season shape: the Jun 29 week ($16,333) is still peak week, ahead of Aug 3 ($11,715). August is not automatically the biggest week of the year here — the July 4 week is.
