## Structural notes
- Weekday-vs-weekend is not a finding; the interesting thing is when the *ratio* changes. Right now weekends are still producing 2.5–4x weekdays (Aug 15 $2,038 vs Aug 18 $475), which means the weekend premium is holding even as the weekday floor collapses. That's consistent with day-trippers persisting while week-long renters have gone.
- Order count is the better health metric than revenue in the shoulder, because AOV rises as the mix shifts from ice/consumables to apparel and hard goods. Revenue can hold flat while footfall halves.
- Weekly channel share sits at 98%+ POS all through peak. Do not report it.
- The expectation curve's day-of-week multiplier makes % -of-expected non-comparable across weekdays. Always state the raw gap alongside.
